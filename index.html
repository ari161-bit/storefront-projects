Sizing & Fit Guide

Accurate measurements ensure your press-ons fit like a second skin. All you need is a soft measuring tape or a strip of paper and a ruler (in millimeters).

01

Wrap the tape

Wrap a soft measuring tape (or a thin paper strip) horizontally across the widest part of your nail bed, from one side to the other.

02

Measure in millimeters

Note the width in mm. If using paper, mark the overlap point, then lay it flat against a ruler.

03

Repeat for all 10 fingers

Record each measurement, thumb, index, middle, ring, and pinky, both hands if they differ.

04

Enter your sizes

Use the Custom Orders form to enter exact mm values, or choose the closest XS–L preset.
